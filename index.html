GiggleGlow Uploader

A small private tool for the GiggleGlow TV channel.

GiggleGlow Uploader helps the owner of the GiggleGlow TV channel publish the short cartoon videos they make. Each day it:

  • emails the owner the creative prompts for the next episode,
  • picks up the finished video and thumbnail that the owner puts in their own Google Drive folder,
  • uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel with its title, description, playlist and schedule,
  • posts the same video as a Reel on the owner's own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page at the same time, and
  • emails the owner a short report.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in, and it acts only on the owner's own Google, Instagram and Facebook accounts.
