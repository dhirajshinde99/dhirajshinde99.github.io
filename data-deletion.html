Data deletion

GiggleGlow Uploader · Effective 10 October 2026

What the app keeps

GiggleGlow Uploader is used only by its owner. It does not collect or store information about anyone else, including people who watch the videos. It keeps only:

  • the owner's sign-in tokens for Google and Meta, stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository, and
  • a small log of published episodes (episode numbers, post IDs and dates) in the same private repository.

Video and image files are processed temporarily and deleted when each run ends.

How to delete it

  1. Facebook and Instagram: in Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, choose GiggleGlow Uploader and remove it. This also ends Instagram access, because the app reaches Instagram through Facebook Login.
  2. Google: remove GiggleGlow Uploader at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. Stored tokens and logs: delete the secrets, or the whole private repository, on GitHub. Nothing else is kept anywhere.

Videos that were already posted stay on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook until the owner deletes them in those apps.

Requests

If you think this app holds information about you, open a request at github.com/dhirajshinde99/dhirajshinde99.github.io/issues. It will be answered, and any such information deleted, within 30 days.
